Methodology and validation

The index combines adjusted Terra (2001–2002), Aqua (2003–2018), and adjusted NOAA-20 (2019–2025). Each adjustment is fitted for the selected country using months when both satellites reported data.

Compare the two error columns to see how much the adjustment changed agreement with Aqua in months held out from fitting. Smaller differences mean closer agreement with Aqua.

Agreement with Aqua measures cross-satellite consistency. Hotspots are detected pixels. Cloud cover, scan geometry, overpass timing and differences in sensitivity can affect the record. Early Terra estimates use a factor fitted on later years.
